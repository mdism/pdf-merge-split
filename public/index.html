    Questions

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Merging and splitting happen in your browser, so your documents never leave your device and nothing is saved.

    Can I choose which pages to keep?

    Yes. In Split, type pages like 1-3, 5 to get one PDF with just those pages, or split every page into its own PDF.

    Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

    Not yet. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then merge or split it here.
